How the calculation works

Coins = position size ÷ entry
Profit = coins × (exit − entry) for a long, coins × (entry − exit) for a short
Fees = coins × (entry + exit) × fee %
Return on margin = profit ÷ (position size ÷ leverage)

Example: $1,000 long at $3,000, sold at $3,300 with a 0.1% fee per side: gross +$100, fees $2.10, profit +$97.90 (+9.79%). With 5x leverage the same trade uses $200 of margin, so the return on your money is +48.95%, and a 20% drop against you would be enough to liquidate it.

Break-even price

Because you pay fees on the way in and out, the price has to move a little before you're even. With 0.1% per side that's about +0.2% for a long.
