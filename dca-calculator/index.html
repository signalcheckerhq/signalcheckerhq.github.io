Why not just average the prices?

Because each buy counts in proportion to the coins it bought. With the example buys ($100 at $100, $100 at $90, $200 at $80), the real average is $86.75 before fees, not $90. The bigger buy at the lower price pulls the average down. Full explanation in our guide How to calculate your DCA average price.

Average price = total spent ÷ total coins bought

What the stop really costs

If every DCA level fills and the stop is hit, you lose on the whole position. The calculator shows that number before you trade, and warns you if your stop sits above your lowest planned buy.
