Why I built these

My trading bot sent me 552 profit emails in a year. It never sent one about the losses. When I finally added everything up, the picture was very different.

So I built tools that show the whole picture: every stop-out, every fee, every losing streak. Read the story.

How it works

  1. Buy on Gumroad or Etsy and get an instant download.
  2. Click your personal link and choose Make a copy. The sheet is now in your own Google Drive.
  3. Paste or type your data. Your numbers never leave your sheet.

Questions

Do I need Excel or any software?

No. Everything runs in free Google Sheets, in your browser. You only need a Google account.

Do you sell trading signals?

No. These tools measure and record. They never tell you what or when to trade, and nothing here is financial advice.

Do I need to connect my exchange?

No. No exchange logins and no API keys. Prices come from public market data.
