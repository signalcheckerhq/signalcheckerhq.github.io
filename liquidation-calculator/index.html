How it's estimated

Long: liquidation ≈ entry × (1 − 1/leverage + maintenance margin)
Short: liquidation ≈ entry × (1 + 1/leverage − maintenance margin)

This is the usual estimate for an isolated-margin position. Exchanges use different maintenance-margin tiers, fees and funding, and cross-margin positions depend on your whole balance, so the real price can differ. Always check the liquidation price your exchange shows.

Leverage doesn't change your risk; it changes your margin

If you size a trade by its stop, the dollar loss at the stop is the same at 2x or 20x. Leverage only changes how much money you lock up and how close liquidation sits. The danger is when liquidation comes before the stop: then you can lose the whole margin instead of your planned risk. Use the free position size calculator to size the trade first.
