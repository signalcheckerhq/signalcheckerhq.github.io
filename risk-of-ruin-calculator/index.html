What "risk of ruin" means here

It's the chance that, somewhere along the next trades, your account falls to the ruin level you set (by default half of what you started with). Most traders don't blow up because their strategy has no edge. They blow up because they risk too much per trade and a normal losing streak arrives.

Why the risk table matters most

Look at how fast the chance of −50% climbs as the risk per trade goes up, with the same win rate. A 45% win rate means 6 or 7 losses in a row will happen sooner or later. At 2% risk that's a painful −12%. At 10% risk it's about −50%.

Where do my numbers come from?

Use your own history: win rate and average win ÷ average loss from your journal, or test a signal group's calls with the Signal Checker demo or the Telegram channel checker. Then size each trade with the position size calculator.
