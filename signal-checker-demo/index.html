What the demo checks

For each call it downloads Coinbase's hourly candles from the hour the call was posted. First it checks whether the price actually reached your entry. Then, hour by hour, whether a target or the stop came first. If both happen inside the same hour, it counts the stop: hourly candles can't tell which came first, and assuming the worst keeps the result honest.

Why groups look better than they are

"TP1 hit ✅" posts rarely mention that price later fell through the stop, or that the entry never filled at all. Read the full checklist in How to check if a crypto signal group is legit.
