  1. Open Telegram Desktop (the phone app can't export).

Why check a whole channel?

A channel's own results posts show the wins. Its message history shows everything: calls whose entry never filled, targets that were hit only after the stop, and calls that quietly disappeared. Checking every call against real prices gives you the number the channel won't post.

What counts as a call

Any message with a coin, an entry, a stop and at least one target, in English, Spanish or Russian. Reposts of the same call are counted once. Updates like "TP1 hit ✅" are skipped, because the check works out what happened on its own. Paste a single message into the Signal Checker demo to see how one call is read.
