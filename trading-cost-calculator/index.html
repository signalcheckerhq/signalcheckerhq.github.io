Three costs, every trade

Fees are charged on the full position, not your margin, at entry and again at exit. Spread and slippage are the gap between the price you see and the price you get, usually worst with market orders on small coins. Funding is paid between longs and shorts on perpetual futures every few hours for as long as the position is open.

Why leverage makes costs bigger

With 10x leverage, a 0.1% round-trip fee is 1% of your margin on every trade. Twenty trades a month is 20% of your margin gone to the exchange before you win or lose anything.

The number that matters: costs vs. your stop

If your stop is 1% away and costs are 0.15%, every loss is really 1.15% and every win is smaller too, so you need a higher win rate to break even. Wider stops with smaller positions, limit orders and fewer trades all bring that share down. Check your win rate and reward:risk with the risk of ruin simulator.
